02 · Problem & audience

"Mars-like" places on Earth: how alike, really?

  • ProblemMissions are rehearsed at analog sites on Earth, yet "similar" is hard to see and compare.
  • Who faces itStudents, educators and space enthusiasts; early field-test planners concept
  • Why it mattersEvery analog matches only some conditions. Missing the gaps leads to wrong conclusions.
  • Challenge linkEarth Analog: compare places on Earth with Mars and the Moon.

03 · Our solution

An atlas that scores the similarity

Pick a place on Earth and see, criterion by criterion, how close it is to a site on Mars or the Moon.

  • 12Earth analog sites
  • 6Mars & Moon reference sites
  • 8similarity criteria
  • 2languages (UA / EN)
  • What we builtA web app with Globe, Map and Analysis views.
  • What is originalIt also shows where the analogy breaks, and how to close the gap.
